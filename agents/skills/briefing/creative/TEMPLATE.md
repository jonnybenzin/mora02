# Template

The target structure of the finished brief. The agent fills it field by field
in the second pass and writes `open` wherever nothing solid exists — never a
plausible value.

Generic and meant to be rewritten, like the question catalogue.

---

```markdown
# Brief: <short title>

**Date:** <date>   ·   **Client:** <who>   ·   **Approver:** <who signs off>

## At a glance
<Two or three sentences: what is being made, for whom, by when. Someone who
reads only this can still take part in the conversation.>

## Occasion
<What set it off.>

## Goal
<What should be different afterwards. Measurable where possible.>

## Audience
<Who sees it, in what situation.>

## Message
<The one sentence that should stick.>

## Format and channel
| What | Dimensions / length | Where |
|---|---|---|
| <format> | <dimensions> | <channel> |

## Tone
<How it should feel. References, positive as well as negative.>

## Mandatory
- <has to appear>

## Excluded
- <must not appear>

## Material
<What exists and where it is.>

## Deadline and constraints
<Deadline, interim stages, budget or effort frame, number of variants.>

## Open
- <field> — <what is still missing and who would have to be asked>
```

---

**On the "Open" section:** it is compulsory and stays in place even when empty
— then with the word "nothing". A template whose gap list disappears as soon as
there are no gaps looks the same for an incomplete brief as for a complete one.
